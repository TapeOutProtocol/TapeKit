屏蔽规则与举报

这个网关只做一件事：把存在 BNB 链上的网站读出来交给你的浏览器。内容不在这台服务器上，我们删不掉链上的任何东西；但网关是展示方，可以拒绝显示。

会被屏蔽的内容

  • 儿童性剥削材料；
  • 恶意软件、钓鱼、伪装成他人（含伪装成钱包或交易所）的页面；
  • 收到有效通知的侵权内容；
  • 网关所在司法辖区法律明确禁止的其它内容。

怎么举报

发邮件到 codongorg@gmail.com，标题写 [tapekit abuse]，正文写：网站的链上名字（例如 4246.0.tape）或子域名、问题是什么、你是谁（版权投诉需要权利证明）。

处理时限与方式

  1. 收到后 24 小时内回复确认；
  2. 核实属实的，48 小时内加入屏蔽名单。名单是公开的静态文件 /.tape/blocklist.txt，屏蔽按链上名字或容器地址，全网 10 分钟内生效；
  3. 被屏蔽的网站打开时显示「此客户端已屏蔽这个网站」，不显示任何内容；
  4. 站长认为屏蔽有误，用同一个邮箱申诉，标题写 [tapekit appeal]。

屏蔽只对这个网关有效。链上数据不会被改动，其它网关和客户端不受影响；网关代码开源，任何人都可以架自己的网关并制定自己的规则。

Blocking policy & reporting

This gateway does one thing: it reads websites stored on BNB Chain and hands them to your browser. The content is not on this server and we cannot delete anything on chain; but the gateway is the party doing the displaying, and it can refuse to display.

What gets blocked

  • Child sexual abuse material;
  • Malware, phishing, and pages impersonating others (including wallets and exchanges);
  • Infringing content after a valid notice;
  • Other content clearly prohibited by the law of the jurisdiction the gateway operates in.

How to report

Email codongorg@gmail.com with the subject [tapekit abuse]. Include the site's on-chain name (for example 4246.0.tape) or subdomain, what the problem is, and who you are (copyright complaints need proof of rights).

Timeline and method

  1. Acknowledgement within 24 hours;
  2. Confirmed reports are added to the blocklist within 48 hours. The list is a public static file, /.tape/blocklist.txt, keyed by on-chain name or container address, effective everywhere within 10 minutes;
  3. A blocked site shows "this client has blocked this site" and no content;
  4. Site owners who believe a block is wrong can appeal at the same address with the subject [tapekit appeal].

Blocking affects this gateway only. On-chain data is untouched and other gateways and clients are unaffected; the gateway is open source, and anyone can run their own with their own rules.
